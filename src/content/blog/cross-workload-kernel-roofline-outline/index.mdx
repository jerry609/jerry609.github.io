## 1. Why a Single Tuned Kernel Collapses Across Workloads

<aside class="sidenote">
  <span class="sn-num">1</span>
  本文叙事结构参考 Thinking Machines Lab 的 <em>Defeating Nondeterminism in LLM Inference</em>：先揭示单点调优在真实 serving 分布下的反直觉退化，再通过第一性物理模型与逐段特化代码把整条优化链路讲透。
</aside>

在 LLM 推理与训练系统里，算子工程师经常遇到一个令人困惑的现象：某个在典型 shape 上跑出 88% HBM 带宽利用率的 CUDA / Triton Kernel，一旦放进完整的 benchmark 集合（同时包含 `B=1` 短上下文交互、`B=64` 高并发吞吐、以及 `128K` 长上下文或变长 ragged 请求），整体几何平均加速比会明显回落，最差 20% 长尾 case 甚至比基线更慢。<span class="sn-ref">1</span>

常见直觉会认为这是“自动调优搜索轮数不够”或“Tile 网格没扫细”。但只要把算子的端到端理想耗时写成三段式物理下界，根因就会立刻浮出水面：

$$
T_{\mathrm{ideal}}(w) = \max\!\left(\tau_{\mathrm{launch}},\;\; \frac{\mathrm{Bytes}(w)}{\mathrm{Peak}_{\mathrm{bw}}},\;\; \frac{\mathrm{FLOPs}(w)}{\mathrm{Peak}_{\mathrm{comp}}}\right)
$$

同一个算子在不同 Workload $w$ 上面对的**物理敌人本身不同**：

- **Memory-bound 家族**：把有效 HBM 带宽利用率逼近 $\mathrm{Peak}_{\mathrm{bw}}$；
- **Compute-bound 家族**：把有效 Tensor / Cube 算力利用率逼近 $\mathrm{Peak}_{\mathrm{comp}}$；
- **Launch / Latency-bound 家族**：小形状下访存和计算时间都小于数微秒，唯一能做的是把总耗时逼近发射与同步地板 $\tau_{\mathrm{launch}}$。

更关键的是，GPU 的寄存器分配、Shared Memory 静态声明，以及 Ascend NPU 的 `TPipe/TQue` Unified Buffer 双缓冲决策，都在**编译期**锁定。强行把三条相反的硬件路径塞进同一个 Kernel 的 `if/else` 里，最重路径的寄存器和片上内存占用会拖垮所有轻路径的 Occupancy。

## 2. Constraint & Performance Analysis: Four Orthogonal Dimensions

<aside class="sidenote">
  <span class="sn-num">2</span>
  在 Horace He 的 <em>Defeating Nondeterminism</em>（Figure 11）中，batch size 连续增大时出现的锯齿状性能抖动（jigsaw pattern），正是 Tile 量化与 Wave Quantization 叠加的直观体现。
</aside>

给定三元组 `(Operator, Workloads, Hardware)`，在动手写任何一行 Kernel 代码之前，先沿四个正交维度做一次静态 + 轻量画像诊断<span class="sn-ref">2</span>：

<ConstraintMatrixFig />

1. **瓶颈类型（Bottleneck Regime）**：对比算术强度 $\mathrm{AI}(w) = \mathrm{FLOPs}(w) / \mathrm{Bytes}(w)$ 与硬件拐点 $I_{\mathrm{ridge}} = \mathrm{Peak}_{\mathrm{comp}} / \mathrm{Peak}_{\mathrm{bw}}$，同时检查 $\max(\mathrm{Bytes}/\mathrm{Peak}_{\mathrm{bw}}, \mathrm{FLOPs}/\mathrm{Peak}_{\mathrm{comp}})$ 是否已落入发射地板 $\tau_{\mathrm{launch}}$ 以内。
2. **SM / Core 占用与波次（Wave Quantization）**：计算网格并行度与芯片核数之比 $\mathrm{wave\_ratio} = N_{\mathrm{blocks}} / N_{\mathrm{cores}}$。当 $\mathrm{wave\_ratio} \ll 1$ 时大量核心空转；当 $\mathrm{wave\_ratio} = 1.15$ 时，第二波（Tail Wave）仅占 15% 核心却拉长近一倍延迟。
3. **负载分布偏度（Load Distribution Skew）**：在变长序列（Ragged / Varlen）或稀疏路由下，全局耗时由最慢的 CTA 决定（$\max_i t(\mathrm{CTA}_i)$），偏度比 $\mathrm{skew\_ratio} = \max(L_i) / \mathrm{mean}(L_i)$ 越大，静态均分调度退化越剧烈。
4. **关键静态维度与片上资源边界（Static Dims & On-Chip Cliffs）**：`head_dim`、对齐粒度（CUDA `16B` vs. Ascend `32B`）与片上容量上限（H100 `228 KB` SMEM/SM vs. Ascend 910 **`192 KB` UB/AIV**）构成硬悬崖——越过阈值轻则触发 Register Spill、占用率腰斩，重则在 NPU 上直接编译/运行报错。

## 3. Case Distribution Analysis: FlashInfer GQA vs. CANN-Bench FlashAttention

下面我们直接拿两个跨平台代表算子做完整的 **Case Distribution 拆解**：

| 硬件平台与基准 | 代表算子 | 峰值算力 (BF16) | HBM 带宽 | 核心数 | 片上内存硬边界 | 发射地板 $\tau_{\mathrm{launch}}$ | 对齐约束 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NVIDIA H100 80GB SXM5** (FlashInfer-Bench) | `gqa_decode` (36 cases) | `989 TFLOPS` | `3,350 GB/s` | `132 SMs` | `228 KB` SMEM / SM | `~4 µs` | `16 B` |
| **Huawei Ascend 910** (CANN-Bench v2.1 L4) | `flash_attention` GQA (20 cases) | `320 TFLOPS` | `1,200 GB/s` | `32 Cores` | **`192 KB` UB / AIV** (`512 KB` L1 / AIC) | `~12 µs` | **`32 B`** |

<WorkloadDistFig />

### 3.1 Case A — FlashInfer-Bench `gqa_decode` on H100 (36 Workloads)

设典型 Grouped-Query Attention 配置为 $H_q = 32,\; H_{kv} = 8$（分组比 $G = H_q / H_{kv} = 4$），头维度 $D = 128$，BF16（2 Bytes）。对单个 Decode step，读取整个 Paged KV Cache 的字节数与计算量近似为：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Bytes}(B, L) &\approx 2 \cdot B \cdot H_{kv} \cdot L \cdot D \cdot 2\;\text{B}, \\
\mathrm{FLOPs}(B, L) &\approx 4 \cdot B \cdot H_q \cdot L \cdot D
\end{aligned}
$$

两者相除得到 GQA Decode 的核心算术强度：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AI}_{\mathrm{GQA\text{-}decode}} &\approx G\;\text{FLOPs/Byte} \\
(G=4 &\Rightarrow 4\text{–}8\;\text{FLOPs/Byte} \ll I_{\mathrm{ridge}}^{\mathrm{H100}} = 295)
\end{aligned}
$$

也就是说，GQA Decode 在宏观上几乎永远不是 Compute-bound，但 36 个 case 在 **(Batch $B$, KV 长度 $L$, 变长偏度)** 平面上仍然剧烈分裂为 **3 个互斥的物理家族**：

| Workload Family | Case 分布特征（36 cases） | 四维诊断画像 | 单 Kernel 为何在此退化 | 最优 Kernel 结构策略 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **F1 · Small-B / Short-KV** | 12 cases: $B \in [1, 8],\; L \in [128, 1024]$ | `Bytes ≈ 0.5–16 MB`，理想访存耗时仅 `0.15–4.8 µs`，与 $\tau_{\mathrm{launch}} \approx 4\;\mu\text{s}$ 同量级；不切分时 `B·H_kv = 8..64 ≪ 132 SMs` | 若强行套用长 KV 的两阶段 Split-KV，多一次 reduction kernel 发射（`+4 µs`）与中间结果写回，直接把总延迟拖慢 20%+ | **`kernel_F1`**：单遍 Warp-Fused GQA，一个 CTA 打包整组 $G=4$ 个 Q heads，零 Split、零全局 workspace、直接写回输出 |
| **F2 · Mid/Large-B Streaming** | 12 cases: $B \in [16, 64],\; L \in [1024, 8192]$ | `B·H_kv = 128..512` 自然打满 `132 SMs`（`wave_ratio ≈ 1.0–3.9`）；完全处于 **Memory-bound ($\mathrm{Peak}_{\mathrm{bw}}$)** 甜区 | 过度 Split-K 反而破坏 L2 缓存局部性、引入额外同步；向量化不足或页表寻址串行化会拖低 HBM3 通道利用率 | **`kernel_F2`**：单阶段 Paged-KV 流式 Kernel，3 级异步流水（`cp.async` / `num_stages=3`），128-bit 合并读 KV 页，每个 KV 块在寄存器内被 $G$ 个 Q head 复用 $G$ 次 |
| **F3 · Long-KV / Ragged Skew** | 12 cases: $B \in [1, 8],\; L \in [16\text{K}, 128\text{K}]$，偏度达 `2.4×` | `B·H_kv = 8..64` 仅占 H100 不到一半 SM，**SM 严重饥饿**；且变长 batch 中单个 `128K` 请求拖死全批同步（Load Skew） | 无 Split-KV 的 F2 方案在长序列上只跑几个 SM，带宽利用率暴跌至 30% 以下 | **`kernel_F3`**：两阶段 **Dynamic Split-KV + LSE Combine**，按序列长度动态计算 `num_splits` 使总块数对齐 `132 SMs` 的整数倍，长请求拆成更多块消除偏度木桶效应 |

### 3.2 Case B — CANN-Bench v2.1 `flash_attention` (GQA) on Ascend 910 (20 Workloads)

切换到华为昇腾 Ascend 910（`Ascend910_9382`）时，硬件约束的重心从“SM 波次”显著转向 **Da Vinci 解耦架构（Cube AIC 与 Vector AIV 分离）+ 严苛的 `192 KB` Unified Buffer 硬容量墙 + `32B` 内存对齐**。20 个 GQA FlashAttention case 按序列长度 $S$ 与头维度 $D$ 分裂为三个家族：

| Workload Family | Case 分布特征（20 cases） | 四维诊断画像 | 单 Kernel 为何在此退化/报错 | 最优 Kernel 结构策略 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **F1 · Short-Seq** | 7 cases: $S \in [128, 512],\; B \in [1, 4]$ | 核并行度不足 32 核，且 $\tau_{\mathrm{launch}} \approx 12\;\mu\text{s}$ 与 `MTE2/MTE3` 管线冷启动占比高 | 过大的 `Br/Bc` 分块导致核间负载不均，重型 Ping-Pong 队列初始化徒增延迟 | **`kernel_F1`**：小分块（`Br=32, Bc=64`）单遍 UB 驻留融合，尾块严格对齐 `32B`（16 个 FP16） |
| **F2 · UB Ping-Pong Sweet Spot** | 7 cases: $S \in [1024, 4096],\; D = 128$ | `Br=64, Bc=64` 下的 `QKᵀ` 得分块与 Softmax 缓冲在 **`BUFFER_NUM = 2`** 时约占 `148 KB < 192 KB`；进入 **Compute-bound ($\mathrm{Peak}_{\mathrm{comp}}$)** 甜区 | 单缓冲无法掩盖 `GM → L1 → UB` 的 MTE 搬运延迟，Cube 单元频繁等数据 | **`kernel_F2`**：开启 **`BUFFER_NUM = 2` Ping-Pong 双缓冲**，让 `MTE2` 搬运、`Cube` 矩阵乘、`Vector` Online Softmax 三级流水完全重叠 |
| **F3 · UB Overflow Cliff** | 6 cases: $S \in [8192, 32768]$ 或 $D = 256$ | 若沿用 F2 的 `BUFFER_NUM = 2` + `Bc=64`，片上工作集突破 `192 KB`（`exceeds_onchip_mem = True`），**直接触发 UB Overflow 硬错误** | 不仅是性能变慢，而是**编译/运行直接失败**；若盲目切太小又会造成核数不足与 MTE 碎片化 | **`kernel_F3`**：关闭 UB 双缓冲（**`BUFFER_NUM = 1`**），收窄 `Bc=32` 保证工作集严格 `< 192 KB`，同时沿 $S$ 维做 **Multi-Core Split-S** 铺满 32 核并做跨核 LSE 归并 |

## 4. Writing Each Specialized Kernel Independently

<RooflineDispatchFig />

下面展示如何把上述物理分析直接翻译为**彼此独立、无相互污染的专门化 Kernel**，最后再由连续谓词路由器组装起来。

### 4.1 H100 GQA Decode — Per-Family Kernels (Triton)

先写 **F1（Launch-Min 单遍融合）** 与 **F2（Paged-KV 多级流水线）**，两者均不需要全局临时缓冲与第二次 Kernel 发射：

```python
import triton
import triton.language as tl

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Family F1: Small-B / Short-KV -> Minimize tau_launch (Single-pass, zero split)
# Each program handles 1 (batch, kv_head) and packs all G query heads together.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
@triton.jit
def gqa_decode_f1_singlepass(
    Q, K_cache, V_cache, PageTable, SeqLens, Out,
    stride_qb, stride_qh, stride_qd,
    stride_kp, stride_kh, stride_kl, stride_kd,
    stride_pt_b, stride_pt_p,
    sm_scale,
    G: tl.constexpr,          # H_q // H_kv (e.g. 4 or 8)
    D: tl.constexpr,          # head_dim (e.g. 128)
    PAGE_SIZE: tl.constexpr,  # e.g. 16
    BLOCK_N: tl.constexpr,    # e.g. 32 (light registers, fast launch)
):
    b = tl.program_id(0)
    kv_h = tl.program_id(1)
    seq_len = tl.load(SeqLens + b)

    # Load all G query heads once into registers: [G, D]
    offs_g = tl.arange(0, G)
    offs_d = tl.arange(0, D)
    q_ptrs = Q + b * stride_qb + (kv_h * G + offs_g[:, None]) * stride_qh + offs_d[None, :] * stride_qd
    q = tl.load(q_ptrs).to(tl.float32) * sm_scale

    m_i = tl.full([G], -float("inf"), dtype=tl.float32)
    l_i = tl.zeros([G], dtype=tl.float32)
    acc = tl.zeros([G, D], dtype=tl.float32)

    for start_n in range(0, seq_len, BLOCK_N):
        offs_n = start_n + tl.arange(0, BLOCK_N)
        mask_n = offs_n < seq_len
        page_idx = tl.load(PageTable + b * stride_pt_b + (offs_n // PAGE_SIZE) * stride_pt_p, mask=mask_n, other=0)
        page_off = offs_n % PAGE_SIZE

        # Read KV once, reuse across all G query heads (G-fold HBM traffic reduction)
        k_ptrs = K_cache + page_idx[:, None] * stride_kp + kv_h * stride_kh + page_off[:, None] * stride_kl + offs_d[None, :] * stride_kd
        v_ptrs = V_cache + page_idx[:, None] * stride_kp + kv_h * stride_kh + page_off[:, None] * stride_kl + offs_d[None, :] * stride_kd
        k = tl.load(k_ptrs, mask=mask_n[:, None], other=0.0).to(tl.float32)
        v = tl.load(v_ptrs, mask=mask_n[:, None], other=0.0).to(tl.float32)

        qk = tl.dot(q, tl.trans(k)) if G >= 16 else tl.sum(q[:, None, :] * k[None, :, :], axis=2)
        qk = tl.where(mask_n[None, :], qk, -float("inf"))

        m_new = tl.maximum(m_i, tl.max(qk, axis=1))
        p = tl.exp(qk - m_new[:, None])
        alpha = tl.exp(m_i - m_new)
        l_i = l_i * alpha + tl.sum(p, axis=1)
        acc = acc * alpha[:, None] + tl.sum(p[:, :, None] * v[None, :, :], axis=1)
        m_i = m_new

    out = (acc / l_i[:, None]).to(Out.dtype.element_ty)
    out_ptrs = Out + b * stride_qb + (kv_h * G + offs_g[:, None]) * stride_qh + offs_d[None, :] * stride_qd
    tl.store(out_ptrs, out)
```

对于 **F2（高并发带宽流式家族）**，利用更大的 `BLOCK_N=64/128`、多级异步流水（`num_stages=3`）与更宽的 Warp 调度，在 `B·H_kv >= 132` 时直接把 HBM3 压向 `3,350 GB/s` 屋顶（代码结构与 F1 同源但以独立编译配置 `num_stages=3, num_warps=4, BLOCK_N=64` 编译为独立符号 `gqa_decode_f2_paged_stream`，避免重流水线寄存器占用污染 F1）。

再看 **F3（超长 KV / 变长偏度家族）**：采用**两阶段 Split-KV + Log-Sum-Exp Combine**，把单个长序列沿 `L` 维切成 `num_splits` 段铺满 132 个 SM：

```python
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Family F3 (Stage 1): Split-KV across SMs to eliminate under-utilization & skew
# Grid = (B, H_kv, num_splits) -> writes partial (MidOut, MidLSE) per split.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
@triton.jit
def gqa_decode_f3_splitkv_stage1(
    Q, K_cache, V_cache, PageTable, SeqLens, MidOut, MidLSE,
    stride_qb, stride_qh, stride_qd,
    stride_kp, stride_kh, stride_kl, stride_kd,
    stride_pt_b, stride_pt_p,
    stride_mo_b, stride_mo_h, stride_mo_s, stride_mo_d,
    stride_lse_b, stride_lse_h, stride_lse_s,
    sm_scale, num_splits,
    G: tl.constexpr, D: tl.constexpr, PAGE_SIZE: tl.constexpr, BLOCK_N: tl.constexpr,
):
    b = tl.program_id(0)
    kv_h = tl.program_id(1)
    s_id = tl.program_id(2)

    seq_len = tl.load(SeqLens + b)
    chunk = tl.cdiv(seq_len, num_splits)
    kv_start = s_id * chunk
    kv_end = tl.minimum(kv_start + chunk, seq_len)

    offs_g = tl.arange(0, G)
    offs_d = tl.arange(0, D)

    if kv_start >= kv_end:
        lse_ptrs = MidLSE + b * stride_lse_b + (kv_h * G + offs_g) * stride_lse_h + s_id * stride_lse_s
        tl.store(lse_ptrs, -float("inf"))
        return

    q = tl.load(Q + b * stride_qb + (kv_h * G + offs_g[:, None]) * stride_qh + offs_d[None, :] * stride_qd).to(tl.float32) * sm_scale
    m_i = tl.full([G], -float("inf"), dtype=tl.float32)
    l_i = tl.zeros([G], dtype=tl.float32)
    acc = tl.zeros([G, D], dtype=tl.float32)

    for start_n in range(kv_start, kv_end, BLOCK_N):
        offs_n = start_n + tl.arange(0, BLOCK_N)
        mask_n = offs_n < kv_end
        page_idx = tl.load(PageTable + b * stride_pt_b + (offs_n // PAGE_SIZE) * stride_pt_p, mask=mask_n, other=0)
        page_off = offs_n % PAGE_SIZE
        k = tl.load(K_cache + page_idx[:, None] * stride_kp + kv_h * stride_kh + page_off[:, None] * stride_kl + offs_d[None, :] * stride_kd, mask=mask_n[:, None], other=0.0).to(tl.float32)
        v = tl.load(V_cache + page_idx[:, None] * stride_kp + kv_h * stride_kh + page_off[:, None] * stride_kl + offs_d[None, :] * stride_kd, mask=mask_n[:, None], other=0.0).to(tl.float32)

        qk = tl.where(mask_n[None, :], tl.sum(q[:, None, :] * k[None, :, :], axis=2), -float("inf"))
        m_new = tl.maximum(m_i, tl.max(qk, axis=1))
        p = tl.exp(qk - m_new[:, None])
        alpha = tl.exp(m_i - m_new)
        l_i = l_i * alpha + tl.sum(p, axis=1)
        acc = acc * alpha[:, None] + tl.sum(p[:, :, None] * v[None, :, :], axis=1)
        m_i = m_new

    # Store normalized partial output + exact log-sum-exp for numerically stable combine
    lse = m_i + tl.log(l_i)
    mo_ptrs = MidOut + b * stride_mo_b + (kv_h * G + offs_g[:, None]) * stride_mo_h + s_id * stride_mo_s + offs_d[None, :] * stride_mo_d
    lse_ptrs = MidLSE + b * stride_lse_b + (kv_h * G + offs_g) * stride_lse_h + s_id * stride_lse_s
    tl.store(mo_ptrs, acc / l_i[:, None])
    tl.store(lse_ptrs, lse)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Family F3 (Stage 2): Numerically stable Log-Sum-Exp reduction across splits
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
@triton.jit
def gqa_decode_f3_lse_combine(
    MidOut, MidLSE, Out,
    stride_mo_b, stride_mo_h, stride_mo_s, stride_mo_d,
    stride_lse_b, stride_lse_h, stride_lse_s,
    stride_ob, stride_oh, stride_od,
    num_splits, MAX_SPLITS: tl.constexpr, D: tl.constexpr,
):
    b = tl.program_id(0)
    h = tl.program_id(1)
    offs_s = tl.arange(0, MAX_SPLITS)
    offs_d = tl.arange(0, D)
    mask_s = offs_s < num_splits

    lse = tl.load(MidLSE + b * stride_lse_b + h * stride_lse_h + offs_s * stride_lse_s, mask=mask_s, other=-float("inf"))
    m_max = tl.max(lse, axis=0)
    w = tl.where(mask_s, tl.exp(lse - m_max), 0.0)
    w = w / tl.sum(w, axis=0)

    mo = tl.load(MidOut + b * stride_mo_b + h * stride_mo_h + offs_s[:, None] * stride_mo_s + offs_d[None, :] * stride_mo_d, mask=mask_s[:, None], other=0.0)
    out = tl.sum(w[:, None] * mo, axis=0).to(Out.dtype.element_ty)
    tl.store(Out + b * stride_ob + h * stride_oh + offs_d * stride_od, out)
```

### 4.2 Ascend 910 FlashAttention (GQA) — UB-Aware Family Kernels (Ascend C)

在 CANN-Bench v2.1 上，**F2 与 F3 的核心结构差异在于编译期模板参数 `BUFFER_NUM`（Ping-Pong 双缓冲 vs. 单缓冲防溢出）与分块宽度 `Bc`**，确保每个 AIV 的 UB 工作集严格低于 `192 KB` 且单次 DMA 对齐 `32B`：

```cpp
// Ascend C kernel template: specialized separately into F2 (BUFFER_NUM=2) and F3 (BUFFER_NUM=1)
template <int32_t BUFFER_NUM, int32_t BR, int32_t BC>
class FlashAttnGqaKernel {
public:
  __aicore__ inline void Init(GM_ADDR q, GM_ADDR k, GM_ADDR v, GM_ADDR out, const FaTilingData &t) {
    // 192 KB UB budget check (enforced at Host Tiling before launch):
    // UB_bytes = BUFFER_NUM * (BR * BC * sizeof(float) + 2 * BR * D * sizeof(half)) <= 192 * 1024
    pipe.InitBuffer(inQueK, BUFFER_NUM, BC * t.alignedD * sizeof(half));   // 32B aligned
    pipe.InitBuffer(inQueV, BUFFER_NUM, BC * t.alignedD * sizeof(half));
    pipe.InitBuffer(vecScoreBuf, BUFFER_NUM, BR * BC * sizeof(float));
    pipe.InitBuffer(outQueO, 1, BR * t.alignedD * sizeof(half));
  }

  __aicore__ inline void Process(const FaTilingData &t) {
    // F2 (BUFFER_NUM=2): MTE2 CopyIn(i+1) overlaps Cube MatMul(i) & Vector OnlineSoftmax(i)
    // F3 (BUFFER_NUM=1): Narrow BC=32 + Multi-Core Split-S across 32 AI Cores -> zero UB overflow
    for (int32_t step = t.kvStart; step < t.kvEnd; step += BC) {
      CopyInKV(step);     // MTE2: GM -> L1/UB (32B aligned burst)
      ComputeQKAndP();    // AIC Cube QK^T -> AIV Vector Online Softmax
      AccumulatePV();     // AIC Cube P*V  -> AIV rescale accumulator
    }
    CopyOutO();           // MTE3: UB -> GM
  }
private:
  AscendC::TPipe pipe;
  AscendC::TQue<AscendC::QuePosition::VECIN, BUFFER_NUM> inQueK, inQueV;
  AscendC::TQue<AscendC::QuePosition::VECOUT, 1> outQueO;
  AscendC::TBuf<AscendC::TPosition::VECCALC> vecScoreBuf;
};

// Host-side Tiling Dispatch instantiates distinct legal binaries per family:
//   F1 (Short-Seq):   FlashAttnGqaKernel</*BUF=*/1, /*BR=*/32, /*BC=*/64>
//   F2 (UB-Fit Mid):  FlashAttnGqaKernel</*BUF=*/2, /*BR=*/64, /*BC=*/64>  // Ping-Pong Peak_comp
//   F3 (UB-Safe Long):FlashAttnGqaKernel</*BUF=*/1, /*BR=*/32, /*BC=*/32>  // Multi-Core Split-S
```

## 5. Continuous-Predicate Router & Final Assembly Code

<aside class="sidenote">
  <span class="sn-num">3</span>
  工程纪律：路由器只允许读取张量维度、偏度与硬件常量等<strong>连续物理谓词</strong>，严禁出现 <code>if workload_id == "case_03"</code> 这类测试集硬编码，并且必须绑定一个经过全量验证的安全 <code>fallback</code> 分支。
</aside>

将独立调优好的 `kernel_F1 / F2 / F3` 组装成一个对外无感、可直接替换原算子的统一入口，需要三步：**4D 轻量画像 → 连续物理谓词路由 → 动态波次对齐参数计算**<span class="sn-ref">3</span>：

```python
import math
from dataclasses import dataclass
import torch

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class H100Specs:
    num_sms: int = 132
    hbm_bw_gbps: float = 3350.0
    smem_kb: float = 228.0
    launch_floor_us: float = 4.0

def choose_wave_aligned_splits(B: int, H_kv: int, max_seq: int, num_sms: int = 132) -> int:
    """Pick num_splits so total CTAs (B * H_kv * splits) align to SM waves."""
    base_ctas = B * H_kv
    if base_ctas >= num_sms:
        return 1
    # Target 1-2 full waves without shrinking each split below 512 KV tokens
    target_splits = math.ceil(num_sms / base_ctas)
    max_useful = max(1, max_seq // 512)
    return max(1, min(16, target_splits, max_useful))

def gqa_decode_dispatch(
    q: torch.Tensor,          # [B, H_q, D]
    k_cache: torch.Tensor,    # [num_pages, H_kv, PAGE_SIZE, D]
    v_cache: torch.Tensor,
    page_table: torch.Tensor, # [B, max_pages]
    seq_lens: torch.Tensor,   # [B]
    hw: H100Specs = H100Specs(),
) -> torch.Tensor:
    B, H_q, D = q.shape
    _, H_kv, PAGE_SIZE, _ = k_cache.shape
    G = H_q // H_kv
    sm_scale = 1.0 / math.sqrt(D)
    out = torch.empty_like(q)

    # Lightweight 4D physical features (continuous predicates, zero case_id hardcoding)
    max_seq = int(seq_lens.max().item())
    mean_seq = float(seq_lens.float().mean().item())
    skew_ratio = max_seq / max(1.0, mean_seq)
    base_ctas = B * H_kv
    wave_ratio = base_ctas / hw.num_sms
    est_bytes = 2.0 * B * H_kv * mean_seq * D * q.element_size()
    est_bw_us = (est_bytes / (hw.hbm_bw_gbps * 1e9)) * 1e6

    # --- Route 1 (F1): Launch / Latency-bound -> single-pass zero-overhead warp kernel ---
    if est_bw_us <= 2.0 * hw.launch_floor_us and max_seq <= 1024:
        gqa_decode_f1_singlepass[(B, H_kv)](
            q, k_cache, v_cache, page_table, seq_lens, out,
            *q.stride(), *k_cache.stride(), *page_table.stride(),
            sm_scale, G=G, D=D, PAGE_SIZE=PAGE_SIZE, BLOCK_N=32,
            num_warps=2, num_stages=1,
        )
        return out

    # --- Route 3 (F3): Long-KV SM under-utilization OR high ragged load skew -> Split-KV ---
    if (wave_ratio < 0.75 and max_seq >= 4096) or (skew_ratio >= 1.6 and max_seq >= 2048):
        num_splits = choose_wave_aligned_splits(B, H_kv, max_seq, hw.num_sms)
        if num_splits > 1:
            max_splits_pow2 = 1 << (num_splits - 1).bit_length()
            mid_out = torch.empty((B, H_q, num_splits, D), device=q.device, dtype=torch.float32)
            mid_lse = torch.empty((B, H_q, num_splits), device=q.device, dtype=torch.float32)

            gqa_decode_f3_splitkv_stage1[(B, H_kv, num_splits)](
                q, k_cache, v_cache, page_table, seq_lens, mid_out, mid_lse,
                *q.stride(), *k_cache.stride(), *page_table.stride(),
                *mid_out.stride(), *mid_lse.stride(),
                sm_scale, num_splits, G=G, D=D, PAGE_SIZE=PAGE_SIZE, BLOCK_N=64,
                num_warps=4, num_stages=2,
            )
            gqa_decode_f3_lse_combine[(B, H_q)](
                mid_out, mid_lse, out,
                *mid_out.stride(), *mid_lse.stride(), *out.stride(),
                num_splits, MAX_SPLITS=max_splits_pow2, D=D,
                num_warps=2,
            )
            return out

    # --- Route 2 (F2 / Verified Safe Fallback): Peak_bw streaming kernel ---
    gqa_decode_f1_singlepass[(B, H_kv)](
        q, k_cache, v_cache, page_table, seq_lens, out,
        *q.stride(), *k_cache.stride(), *page_table.stride(),
        sm_scale, G=G, D=D, PAGE_SIZE=PAGE_SIZE, BLOCK_N=64,
        num_warps=4, num_stages=3,   # F2 multi-stage async streaming specialization
    )
    return out
```
